MEET THE FINDER

He isn’t retired from finding. He’s finally free to roam.

Andy Smith has spent years spotting, buying, trading, and selling interesting things. Now he’s got more road in front of him and more time to pull over when something looks promising.

He looks for old advertising, paper, tools, signs, collectibles, unusual household pieces, forgotten objects, and the occasional thing that simply feels too good to leave behind.

“The fun is in noticing what other people overlook.”
