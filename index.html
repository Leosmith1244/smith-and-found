MEET THE FINDER

He isn't retired from finding. He's finally free to roam.

Andy Smith has spent years buying, spotting, trading and selling interesting things. These days he has more freedom, more road ahead of him, and no particular reason to drive past an estate sale.

He looks for old advertising, paper, tools, signs, collectibles, unusual household pieces, forgotten objects and the occasional thing that simply feels too good to leave behind.

The point isn't to own everything. The fun is in noticing what other people overlook, learning the story, and finding the right home for the right piece.
